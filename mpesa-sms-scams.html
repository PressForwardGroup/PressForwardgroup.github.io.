How fake M-Pesa and SMS scams work

Common tricks and how to avoid them.

Scammers send messages that look real, often saying money was sent by mistake or that your account has a problem, and pressure you to act fast.

Their goal is to get your PIN, a code, or a payment from you.

Key points

  • Never share your PIN or any code you receive
  • Do not act under pressure, take a moment to check
  • Check the real transaction in your own message history or app
  • Do not click links in unexpected messages
  • Report suspicious messages to your mobile money provider
