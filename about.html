About PressForward Tech Hub

Who we are, what we do, why we do it, and how it helps you.

Who we are

PressForward Tech Hub is a Nairobi-based technology team started in 2025. We secure networks, build websites and web apps, install networks and CCTV, and repair phones, laptops and electronics.

Each service is run by its own department manager, so the person leading your job knows that area well. We also build software for schools and property owners.

What we do, why, and how it helps you

Every service exists to solve a real problem for you.

Cybersecurity

What we do: We check networks, devices and accounts for weak spots, set up protection and backups, and train staff.

Why we do it: One hacked account or lost file can stop a business, and most attacks use simple gaps that can be closed.

How it helps you: You lose less money and time, protect your customers' data, and know what to do if something goes wrong.

Learn more

Web Development

What we do: We build websites and web apps for businesses and organizations, including school and property systems.

Why we do it: Customers look you up online first, and many jobs still done on paper or spreadsheets are faster in one system.

How it helps you: More people can find and contact you, and your team saves time on repeated work.

Learn more

Networking & Installation

What we do: We install and set up Wi-Fi, CCTV, cabling and office or lab networks.

Why we do it: A weak network slows everyone down, and an unwatched building is at risk.

How it helps you: Reliable internet in every room, cameras you can check from your phone, and a neat network that is easy to fix.

Learn more

Phone & Laptop Repairs

What we do: We diagnose and repair phones and laptops, upgrade them and recover data.

Why we do it: A device problem should not mean buying a new one or losing your files.

How it helps you: You save money, get working again quickly, and get honest advice on repairing or replacing.

Learn more

Electronic Appliance Repairs

What we do: We repair home and office electronics such as TVs and sound systems.

Why we do it: Many faults can be fixed, and repair is often cheaper than replacing.

How it helps you: You get a clear price before work starts and a tested repair.

Learn more
