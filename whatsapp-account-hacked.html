What to do if your WhatsApp is hacked

Steps to get your account back and keep it safe.

Attackers often trick people into sharing the 6-digit verification code. With it they take over the account and message your contacts.

Key points

  • Never share your WhatsApp verification code with anyone
  • Reinstall WhatsApp and register your number again to get back in
  • Turn on two-step verification in settings
  • Warn your contacts not to send money or codes
  • Contact us if you need help securing your accounts
