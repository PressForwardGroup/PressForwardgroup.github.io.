Signs your phone needs repair

Problems worth fixing early.

Small faults often get worse. Catching them early can save money and your data.

Key points

  • Battery drains very fast or shuts off suddenly
  • Screen is cracked or touch is not working
  • Phone will not charge properly
  • Phone was in water: switch it off and do not charge it
  • Battery looks swollen: stop using it and get it checked
